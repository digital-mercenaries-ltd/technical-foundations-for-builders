# Editorial history and evidence

This page records completed milestone decisions and points to their review evidence. [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/digital-mercenaries-ltd/technical-foundations-for-builders/issues) is the canonical delivery ledger. This historical record does not state current work status or replace the issue discussion.

## Milestone decisions

Milestone 2 was approved on 2026-07-17 with a provisional 91-entry first traversal. It established the progressive-disclosure structure, dated landscape material and qualified use of practitioner stories and hacker folklore.

Milestone 3 was approved on 2026-07-17. It:

- accepted the Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 first-pass pages;
- accepted three splits that raised the traversal to 94 entries, still below the 95-entry planning guardrail;
- moved binary and hexadecimal to Chapter 1 further territory without rewriting their explanations; and
- retained eight entries as a calibration maximum rather than a target for later chapters.

The Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 Milestone 4 batches were approved on 2026-07-18. Chapter 5 was approved on 2026-07-31. On 2026-07-31, the Chapter 6 controlled increment completed research, drafting, independent review and deterministic checks and entered its owner-review gate.
